PURIN...

(ポムポムプリン, Pomupomupurin) also referred to as Purin or Pompom, is a dog character introduced by the Japanese company Sanrio and is the main protagonist of the Pompompurin universe. A yellow Golden Retriever, Purin was born on a sunny day on April 16th. Pompompurin lives in his own basket in the entrance hall of his sister-owner's house. His trademark is his brown beret that is always on top of his head. Pompompurin's favorite food is his mama's pudding. He made his first appearance in 1996, the same year as Chococat, and is currently one of Sanrio's flagship characters. Pompompurin's interest is collecting shoes, which includes his owner-father's leather shoes, one of his owner-mother's sandal, etc. He likes to hide them. Pompompurin is a laidback individual who loves to make friends, likes soft stuff, and is capable of doing 'purin aerobics'. However, he is quite popular among fans for not only his cute looks, but having a huge appetite for food much more so than other mainline Sanrio characters including fellow dog Cinnamoroll and often spends time sleeping, which is easily showcased in Hello Kitty and Friends Supercute Adventures, and for the latter, one could say he has a talent for it! This itself possibly explains his large but huggable size comparable to Tuxedo Sam and Baku, and he dreams of becoming even bigger. In terms of meals, although he can actually eat almost anything, Pompompurin loves drinking milk and eating cream caramel pudding that his mother makes. Despite being anthropomorphic, as he is a dog, Pompompurin perks up when he hears his owners say "Let's go out!" and he'd rather not hear "Stay!" Pompompurin's best friends include a hamster called Muffin, a mouse named Scone, and a bird named Custard who loves to steal his hat and perch on his head. He secretly likes it when the wind blows Pompompurin's beret off, so he can retrieve it!
